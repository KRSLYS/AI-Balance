Every AI plan and limit, in one place.

AI Balance shows how much of each AI subscription you have used: session and weekly limits, credits and balances. It warns you before a limit runs out.

Download Source code

Windows · macOS · Linux · Android · iOS. Free and open source.

What it does

One card per account, a bar per limit, and the time until it resets.

All your accounts

Add several accounts for the same provider. They sit together, each with its own plan and limits.

A warning in time

Choose a threshold and get a notification when a limit reaches it.

Sign in with a link

Open the sign-in link, approve, and the app finishes by itself. Nothing to type for most providers.

Private by design

Your sign-ins stay on your device and go only to their own provider. There is no AI Balance server.

12 languages

English, Arabic, Chinese and nine European languages, with light and dark themes.

In your system tray

On desktop the fullest limits are one glance away, and the app can start with your computer.

Supported providers

Every number comes from the provider itself. AI Balance estimates nothing.
